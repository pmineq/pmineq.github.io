# Zencut landing assets

hero.jpg, custom.jpg, moving.jpg and print.jpg are official Zencut Korean App Store screenshots, retrieved 2026-11-19 using https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6816699757&country=kr . Store: https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/zencut/id6816699757 . Displayed photo excerpts in the print animation come from these screenshots, not stock or generated photography.

The four basic frame WebP images are resized, web-optimized copies from ios/Zencut/Resources/Frames/.

The moving-cut switch changes official screenshots and animates the shutter; it does not play or claim to play an actual recording.

Story print photo assets: docs/release/app-store-localized/inputs/photo-1.png through photo-4.png in the app project. Print geometry matches StoryCutLayout.swift (1080x1920 canvas, 459x612 slots at x76/y154, 10px gaps); pink grid follows ImageComposer.drawStoryPrintGrid.
